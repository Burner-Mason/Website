Burner Mason is two friends who met in college and went into business together — making lip and hand care by hand, in Washington, with nothing in it we couldn't explain to you.

We're not a brand with a backstory department. We're two people who became friends in college, kept making things together afterward, and turned that into a small lip- and hand-care company we run ourselves.

It started the way these things usually do — with something we wanted and couldn't find. We were tired of lip and hand care that promised a lot on the label and did less in your pocket: fragrance for the sake of fragrance, filler to bulk it out, and packaging you pay for and throw away. So we set out to make our own, and we didn't stop until it actually worked.

01 We make it ourselves

Every balm, oil, salve, and bottle is mixed and poured by hand by the two of us, here in Washington. We work in small batches and make them often, so what arrives in your mailbox is fresh — not something that sat on a pallet for a year.

02 Natural materials, nothing extra

Our formulas are built from natural materials — plant oils, butters, and waxes — and then we stop. No fragrance theater, no filler, no label to peel off. What you pay for is what's inside, not the sticker on the outside.

We'd rather get the formula right than get the sticker right.

03 A year of getting it right

None of these are first drafts. We spent more than a year reformulating and testing through real Washington winters and summers until each one earned its place: a Winter Balm that won't freeze stiff in a coat pocket, a Summer Balm that won't melt in a hot car, an everyday balm for in between, a lip oil for shine, hand salves for day and for night, and a sanitizer that cleans without wrecking your hands.

04 Small on purpose

We keep this small because small is how it stays good. If you're reading this, you're exactly who we make it for — someone who'd rather have the formula than the packaging. It's still just the two of us back here, so every order genuinely lands with the people who made it. Thank you for that.
