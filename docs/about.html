Our story

Made by hand,
made to work.

Burner Mason is two people, a stove, and a stubborn belief that the good stuff doesn't need a label. We make lip and hand care that does exactly one thing each — well — and we leave out everything else.

Made by hand in Washington

Every balm, oil, salve, and bottle of sanitizer is mixed, poured, and packed by the two of us, here in Washington State. We work in small batches and make them often, so nothing sits around going stale before it reaches you.

Natural materials, nothing extra

We build our formulas from natural materials — plant oils, butters, and waxes — and stop there. No fragrance theater, no filler, no labels to throw away. What you pay for is what's inside the tube, not the sticker on the outside.

A year of getting it right

None of these are first drafts. We spent more than a year reformulating and field-testing through real Washington winters and summers until each one earned its place: a balm that won't freeze stiff in your pocket, one that won't melt in a hot car, an everyday balm, a lip oil for shine, salves for day and for night, and a sanitizer that cleans without wrecking your hands.

For our patrons

We keep things small on purpose. If you're reading this, you're the kind of patron we make this for — someone who'd rather have the formula than the packaging. Thank you for that.

— Burner Mason
